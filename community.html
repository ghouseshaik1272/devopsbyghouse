Community guidelines

🤝 Be welcoming and respectful.

🔐 Never share passwords, keys, private client data, or confidential work information.

🧠 Explain what you tried and what you learned.

🚫 Spam, harassment, and abuse are not welcome.

Moderation & safety

In the production version, admins will be able to remove posts/comments and restrict accounts. Regular members will be able to read and write, but not delete other people's content. These controls must be enforced by the server—not just hidden in the page.
